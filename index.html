Meet Ashley

Honest about the things people rarely say out loud.

Ashley Russo has spent 15 years helping professionals, especially in tech, feel confident about the decisions that shape their lives. She's a speaker, the author of F*** You Money, Honey, an EqualAI board member, and the host of Ask Ashley, where she talks with ERG leaders, people leaders and founders about leading with empathy through layoffs, AI and constant change.

“Whether I'm advising tech professionals, partnering with ERG leaders, serving on an AI board or interviewing a leader, I'm driven by one belief: in a time of constant change, it's people who keep society strong for generations to come.”
